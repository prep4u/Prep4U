I.Q Quiz

Test your knowledge on core IQ topics including logic, spatial reasoning, pattern recognition, and problem-solving. Perfect for anyone preparing for cognitive assessments or simply looking to challenge their mind.

Choose the best answer for each multiple-choice question and click "Submit All" at the end to see your results.

1. A school bus leaves Kathmandu at 6:00 a.m. The number of students boarding at four stops is 8, 12, 16, 20. If the pattern continues, how many students board at the fifth stop?

a. 22
b. 24
c. 26
d. 28

2. Rina, Sita, and Mina each live in a different city: Pokhara, Biratnagar, and Lalitpur. Rina does not live in Pokhara. Sita lives in Lalitpur. Where does Mina live?

a. Pokhara
b. Lalitpur
c. Biratnagar
d. Kathmandu

3. A shopkeeper in Bhaktapur has 500 rupees. He spends 175 rupees on groceries and 125 rupees on stationery. How much money remains?

a. Rs. 150
b. Rs. 175
c. Rs. 200
d. Rs. 225

4. Mountain : Climb :: River : ?

a. Read
b. Swim
c. Write
d. Draw

5. Look at the sequence: ▲ ● ▲ ● ▲ ? Which symbol should come next?

a. ▲
b. ■
c. ●
d. ◆

6. Which word does not belong with the others?

a. Kathmandu
b. Pokhara
c. Dharan
d. Mustang

7. A student is facing north. He turns right, then turns right again, and finally turns left. Which direction is he facing?

a. North
b. East
c. South
d. West

8. Find the missing number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

a. 36
b. 42
c. 48
d. 54

9. A student has 3 red pencils, 4 blue pencils, and 2 green pencils. Without looking, what is the minimum number of pencils the student must pick to guarantee getting two pencils of the same color?

a. 2
b. 3
c. 4
d. 5

10. At a school in Chitwan, all members of the science club wear a blue badge. Ram is wearing a blue badge. What can be concluded?

a. Ram must be in the science club.
b. Ram cannot be in the science club.
c. Ram may or may not be in the science club.
d. Nobody in the science club wears a blue badge.

11. A family has Rs. 1,000. They spend Rs. 250 on a bus journey and Rs. 350 on food. They then receive Rs. 200 from a relative. How much money do they have now?

a. Rs. 500
b. Rs. 550
c. Rs. 600
d. Rs. 650

12. Which number completes the pattern?
2 → 5
3 → 7
4 → 9
6 → ?

a. 11
b. 12
c. 13
d. 14

13. During Dashain, a family visits relatives in a certain order. First they visit Hari, then Gita. They visit Mina after Gita but before Suman. Who do they visit third?

a. Hari
b. Gita
c. Mina
d. Suman

14. A rectangular school playground has a gate on its east side. A student enters through the gate and walks straight across the playground toward the opposite side. In which direction is the student walking?

a. North
b. South
c. East
d. West

15. A road from one village to another is 18 km long. A cyclist has already traveled 7 km. He stops after every 3 km for a short rest. How many kilometers does he still need to travel to reach the village?

a. 9 km
b. 10 km
c. 11 km
d. 12 km
