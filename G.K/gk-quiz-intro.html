General Knowledge Quiz Set Practice

General Knowledge (GK) in Nepal is an essential subject for students and aspirants preparing for various competitive exams, including the Lok Sewa Aayog (Public Service Commission) examinations. It encompasses a broad range of topics related to Nepal’s history, geography, polity, economy, culture, and current affairs. Mastery of GK helps candidates perform well in exams, interviews, and enhances their overall awareness of the nation and its global context.


Purpose and Benefits

  • • Knowledge Enhancement
    GK in Nepal reinforces understanding of key national facts, promoting awareness of Nepal’s socio-political and cultural heritage.
  • • Exam Readiness
    Vital for candidates appearing in Lok Sewa exams, civil service tests, and other government recruitment processes.
  • • Self-Assessment
    Allows aspirants to evaluate their strengths and weaknesses on essential subjects, enabling targeted preparation.
  • • Competitive Edge
    Provides a solid foundation for answering objective and descriptive questions related to Nepal’s governance, development, and international relations.

Common Topics in Nepal GK Quiz Sets

❖ History of Nepal

  • - Ancient and medieval history (Licchavi, Malla periods)
  • - Unification of Nepal by Prithvi Narayan Shah
  • - Modern political movements and democratic developments

❖ Geography of Nepal

  • - Physical features: Mountains, rivers, and plains (Terai)
  • - Climate zones and biodiversity
  • - Provinces, districts, and major cities

❖ Political System and Governance

  • - Constitution of Nepal 2015
  • - Structure of government: Executive, legislature, judiciary
  • - Local governance and federalism
  • - Important political leaders and parties

❖ Economy of Nepal

  • - Key sectors: Agriculture, tourism, industry
  • - Economic indicators and development challenges
  • - Foreign aid and remittances

❖ Culture and Society

  • - Languages, religions, festivals, and traditions
  • - Ethnic groups and social diversity
  • - National symbols and heritage sites

❖ Current Affairs

  • - Recent government policies and programs
  • - Nepal’s foreign relations, especially with India, China, and neighboring countries
  • - Major national events and international participation

❖ Science and Technology in Nepal

  • - Innovations and developments
  • - Important institutions and research centers

❖ Miscellaneous

  • - Sports achievements
  • - Famous personalities from Nepal
  • - Awards and recognitions

Sample Quiz Questions

1. Who was the first king to unify Nepal?

  • a) Prithvi Narayan Shah
  • b) Tribhuvan Bir Bikram Shah
  • c) Birendra Bir Bikram Shah
  • d) Rana Bahadur Shah

2. What is the highest mountain peak in Nepal?

  • a) Annapurna
  • b) Everest
  • c) Dhaulagiri
  • d) Manaslu

3. Which year was the current Constitution of Nepal promulgated?

  • a) 2006
  • b) 2015
  • c) 2018
  • d) 2013

4. Nepal shares its longest border with which country?

  • a) China
  • b) India
  • c) Bhutan
  • d) Bangladesh

5. Identify the correct statement about Nepal’s federal structure:

  • a) Nepal has 5 provinces
  • b) Nepal is a unitary state
  • c) Nepal has 7 provinces
  • d) Nepal has 9 provinces

Conclusion

General Knowledge in Nepal is a crucial component for exam success and informed citizenship. Consistent practice with quizzes and study materials tailored to the Lok Sewa syllabus enables candidates to stay updated and confident. Whether you are a first-time exam taker or preparing for a promotion, a strong grasp of Nepal’s history, geography, polity, economy, and current affairs is indispensable. Engaging with GK content regularly helps sharpen reasoning, quick decision-making, and broadens your perspective on Nepal and the world.
