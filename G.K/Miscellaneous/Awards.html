Awards and Recognitions in Nepal

1. National Awards and Honors

A. Nepal Ratna Man Padavi

  • The highest civilian award of Nepal, given by the government to individuals for exceptional contributions to the nation in various fields such as politics, literature, science, social service, and arts.
  • Awarded rarely and considered a great honor.

B. Rastra Gaurav Padak (National Pride Medal)

  • Awarded to individuals or organizations for outstanding service to the country.

C. Suprabal Jana Sewa Shree

  • Awarded for distinguished public service and contributions to the development of Nepal.

D. Maha Ujwaol Rastradeep

  • High-level decoration conferred upon prominent leaders, including former monarchs and political figures.

2. Literary and Cultural Awards

A. Madan Puraskar

  • Nepal’s most prestigious literary award, given annually to the best book published in the Nepali language.
  • Established in 1955 in memory of General Madan Shumsher JBR.
  • Encourages Nepali literature and recognizes outstanding literary works.

B. Sajha Puraskar

  • Awarded by Sajha Publications for excellence in Nepali literature.

C. Bhupi Puraskar

  • Honors poets who have contributed significantly to Nepali poetry.

D. Gopal Das Journalism Award

  • Given for excellence in journalism and media contributions.

3. Sports Awards and Recognitions

A. Nepal Sports Journalists’ Forum Awards

  • Annual awards recognizing achievements in national and international sports.

B. National Sports Awards

  • Given by the Ministry of Youth and Sports to outstanding athletes, coaches, and sports promoters.
  • Categories include Best Male Athlete, Best Female Athlete, Best Coach, and Lifetime Achievement Award.

C. ANFA Awards

  • Presented by the All Nepal Football Association to recognize players, coaches, and clubs excelling in football.

4. International Recognitions

A. CNN Hero of the Year

  • Awarded to Anuradha Koirala in 2010 for her work against human trafficking through Maiti Nepal.

B. Guinness World Records

  • Chandra Bahadur Dangi recognized as the shortest adult man.
  • Various Sherpas and mountaineers have set records for fastest Everest ascents and high-altitude feats.

C. Padma Awards (India)

  • Some Nepali personalities, especially from ethnic groups straddling the Nepal-India border, have been honored with India’s Padma awards for their contribution in arts, literature, and social service.

5. Academic and Scientific Awards

A. Nepal Academy of Science and Technology (NAST) Awards

  • Recognizes scientific research, innovation, and contributions in STEM fields.

B. Tribhuvan University Gold Medals

  • Awarded to top-performing graduates in various faculties.

6. Special Recognitions

A. Honorary Doctorates

  • Several Nepali scholars, artists, and social workers have received honorary doctorates from national and international universities.

B. Mountaineering Honors

  • Sherpas and climbers receive special recognition from mountaineering organizations worldwide for their guiding expertise and rescue efforts.
