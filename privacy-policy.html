Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2025

At Prep4u, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website. We are committed to protecting any personal or non-personal data collected during your visit. Whether you're browsing our content, accessing resources, or interacting with features on our site, we want you to understand how your information is treated. This policy outlines what data we collect, how we use it, how we keep it secure, and the choices you have regarding your information. By continuing to use our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. You can access and use Prep4u completely anonymously, without providing any identifiable details. Our platform is designed to prioritize your privacy, ensuring that you can freely explore our resources without concerns about data collection. We do not require registration, login, or the submission of any personal credentials to access quizzes, study materials, or other content. Your interaction with Prep4u remains entirely private and secure.

2. Cookies

We do not use cookies to track your activity. Your browsing experience on Prep4u is designed to be private and free from third-party tracking. Any temporary data—such as your quiz scores, selected answers, or progress—is stored only in your browser’s memory or local storage. This means the information stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with anyone else. Once you leave the site or close your browser, this data may be cleared automatically, depending on your browser settings. Our goal is to provide a safe, anonymous learning experience without compromising your privacy.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website may include links to third-party sites for your convenience or as part of our educational resources. Please be aware that these external websites operate independently and are not governed by Prep4u’s Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of those websites. If you choose to visit any third-party links, we encourage you to review their privacy policies and terms of use to understand how your information may be collected or used. Your interaction with those sites is solely at your discretion and risk.

4. Children's Privacy

Prep4u does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Our platform is designed to support learning and boost exam confidence, particularly for students, but we do not require users to provide any personal data to access our content. All pages on this site are freely accessible without the need for registration or data submission. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our records.
