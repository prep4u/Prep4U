🧭 Direction Sense Test – Logical Reasoning

✅ What is Direction Sense Test?

The Direction Sense Test evaluates your ability to track directions and calculate distances or final positions based on a series of movements.

It tests:

  • • Orientation ability
  • • Right/left understanding
  • • Spatial visualization
  • • Logical deduction

📚 Types of Direction Sense Questions

🔹 1. Simple Movement-based Questions

Example: A person walks 5 km North, then turns right and walks 3 km. In which direction is he facing?

Answer: East ✅

🔹 2. Distance between Start and End Point

Example: A walks 4 km North, turns left, walks 3 km, turns left again, walks 4 km. How far is he from the starting point?

Answer: 3 km ✅

🔹 3. Final Direction

Example: A starts walking South, turns left, then left again. What is his final direction?

Answer: North ✅

🔹 4. Complex Paths (Multi-turn)

Example: Ram goes 10 m North, then 5 m East, then 6 m South. How far and in what direction is he from the starting point?

Answer: ~6.4 m in North-East direction ✅

🔹 5. Shadow and Time-Based Direction

Example: In the morning, if a man’s shadow falls to his left, which direction is he facing?

Answer: North ✅


🧠 Key Tricks and Shortcuts

Tip Use
👆 Up = North Helps visualize
👇 Down = South Common in drawing
👉 Right turn = Clockwise  
👈 Left turn = Anti-clockwise  
📐 Use 90° turns Most questions involve 90°
📏 Use Pythagoras For diagonal distance
✍️ Always draw the path Reduces error

🧩 Practice Questions

Q1:

A person walks 10 m North, then turns right and walks 5 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. Which direction is he facing?

Answer: South ✅

Q2:

Ravi starts from point A, walks 6 km North to B, turns right and walks 8 km to C, then turns right and walks 6 km to D. How far is he from A?

Answer: 8 km ✅

Q3:

Sunita faces East. She turns left and walks 10 m, then turns right and walks 5 m. Which direction is she facing now?

Answer: East ✅


🎓 Common Errors to Avoid

Mistake Tip
Confusing left/right Visualize facing direction
Not drawing paths Always draw simple arrows
Forgetting net distance Use subtraction
Wrong shadow logic Morning: shadow west, Evening: east
