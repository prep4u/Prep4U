Mirror and Water Images

🔷 Mirror and Water Images – Non-Verbal Reasoning

✅ What is a Mirror Image?

A mirror image is the reflection of an object or figure as seen in a mirror placed vertically on the left or right side of the figure.


✅ What is a Water Image?

A water image is the reflection of an object as seen in a mirror placed horizontally below the figure—just like the reflection you see in water.


🧠 Differences Between Mirror and Water Image


Feature Mirror Image Water Image
Mirror Placement Vertical (left or right side) Horizontal (bottom)
Direction Left ↔ Right changes Top ↔ Bottom changes
Examples b ↔ d, E ↔ Ǝ M ↔ W, P ↔ b

🔍 How to Solve Mirror Image Questions

✅ Steps to Draw Mirror Image

  • Imagine a mirror on the left side of the image.
  • Reverse left and right parts of the figure.
  • Do NOT flip the top and bottom.
  • Letters and numbers appear flipped horizontally.

💧 Water Image Explanation

✅ Steps to Draw Water Image

  • Imagine water below the image.
  • Flip the image upside down (top becomes bottom).
  • Left and right remain the same.

🧩 Sample Questions

🔷 Q1: Mirror Image

What will be the mirror image of “LOG”?
✅ Answer: ⅃ O ϱ

💧 Q2: Water Image

What is the water image of “MATH”?
✅ Answer: W ∀ ⊥ H


📐 Important Tips

Tip # Trick
1️⃣ Only reverse left-right in mirror images
2️⃣ Only reverse top-bottom in water images
3️⃣ Draw grid or trace shapes for tough figures
4️⃣ Practice alphabets and digits separately
5️⃣ Practice mirrored clocks, figures, and symbols too
