🧠 Arithmetic Puzzles

📌 What Are Arithmetic Puzzles?

Arithmetic puzzles are brain teasers or problems that involve numbers and basic arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, or a combination of these.

They test your:

  • • Numerical reasoning
  • • Logical thinking
  • • Problem-solving skills

Unlike straightforward calculations, arithmetic puzzles require you to identify patterns, relationships, or hidden rules to find the solution.


🎯 Why Are Arithmetic Puzzles Important?

  • • Enhance Mental Math: Improve calculation speed and accuracy.
  • • Boost Logical Thinking: Challenge you to think beyond formulas and rules.
  • • Improve Concentration: Require focus to identify subtle patterns.
  • • Popular in Exams: Common in competitive exams, interviews, and IQ tests.

📚 Common Types of Arithmetic Puzzles

  1. • Number and Operation Puzzles: Find the missing number or operation.
    Example: 3 + ? = 10
  2. • Magic Squares: Arrange numbers in a grid so sums of rows, columns, and diagonals are equal.
  3. • Cross-number Puzzles: Like crossword puzzles but with arithmetic clues.
  4. • Digit-Based Puzzles: Involve manipulation like reversing or summing digits.
  5. • Arithmetic Expressions: Find values that make an expression true or balance equations.

🛠️ Strategies for Solving Arithmetic Puzzles

  • • Step 1: Understand the Problem – Identify what’s given and what’s asked.
  • • Step 2: Look for Patterns – Check for symmetry or arithmetic sequences.
  • • Step 3: Break Down the Problem – Divide complex problems into smaller parts.
  • • Step 4: Use Logical Reasoning – Apply arithmetic rules and logic.
  • • Step 5: Verify Solutions – Ensure all conditions are met.

🧩 Examples of Arithmetic Puzzles

Example 1: Find the Missing Number

2, 6, 12, 20, ?

Solution: Differences: 4, 6, 8 → next difference is 10 → 20 + 10 = 30


Example 2: Magic Square (3x3)

Use numbers 1 to 9 so that each row, column, and diagonal sums to 15.


Example 3: Digit Puzzle

The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. The difference between the digits is 3.

Solution: Let digits be x and y

  • x + y = 9
  • |x - y| = 3

Possible solutions: (6,3) or (3,6) → Numbers: 63 or 36


📝 Practice Questions

  1. 1. What comes next? 5, 10, 20, 40, ?
  2. 2. In a magic square of order 3, what should be the sum of each row, column, and diagonal if numbers 1 to 9 are used?
  3. 3. A number increased by 15 equals twice the number decreased by 5. Find the number.
  4. 4. Find the missing digits: 7_5 + 4_9 = 1234

✅ Answers

  1. 1. 80 (Each number is multiplied by 2)
  2. 2. 15 (Sum of 1 to 9 = 45 → 45/3 rows = 15)
  3. 3. Let x = number → x + 15 = 2(x - 5) → x = 25
  4. 4. 795 + 439 = 1234, so missing digits are 9 and 3

💡 Tips to Master Arithmetic Puzzles

  • • Practice regularly with different puzzle types.
  • • Strengthen your basic arithmetic.
  • • Train your brain for pattern recognition.
  • • Read the puzzle carefully before solving.
  • • Try solving mentally to improve speed.
  • • Use scratch paper for complicated calculations.
